

# Activity Instructions

Practice the principle of abstraction by creating classes to represent a resume or an job history for a person like you might see on LinkedIn.

### Design the Classes

![UML design showing two classes: Resume and Job, along with their attributes and respective methods](/images/prep_01_uml.png)

The `Resume` class has an aggregation relationship with the `Job` class. This is because a `Resume` can exist independently without being associated with a `Job`.
